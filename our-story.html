OUR STORY

One name. Two distinct stays.

Hotel Alexandria began welcoming guests in Ntra Hills in 2019. Today, the original hotel continues to serve travellers in Accra while a new chapter takes shape in Klagon Hills: a purpose-built five-star Hotel Alexandria, opening October 2027.
