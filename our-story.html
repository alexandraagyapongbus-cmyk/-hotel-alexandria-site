OUR STORY

One name. A new chapter.

Hotel Alexandria began welcoming guests in Tantra Hills in 2019. Today, the original hotel continues to serve travellers in Accra while a new chapter takes shape in Klagon Hills: a purpose-built five-star Hotel Alexandria, opening October 2027.

2027 · Where Alexandria is going

Two separate destinations, not a renovation — Tantra Hills continues to welcome guests exactly as it does today, while Klagon Hills rises as an entirely new address.

2019
TANTRA HILLS OPENED

Hotel Alexandria opened its doors in Tantra Hills, Accra, with a simple idea: make every guest feel at home. It remains open and welcoming guests today.

KLAGON HILLS OPENS

A purpose-built, five-star Hotel Alexandria rises on Klagon Hills — a new chapter, not a replacement for what came before.
